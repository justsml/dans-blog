# 14. Date Setters Part 5

Which local calendar date does this object represent? Console display formatting may vary.

  const d = new Date(2020, 0, 1)
  d.setMonth(-1)
  console.log(d)
------

1. Jan 01 2020
2. Feb 01 2020
3. Jan 01 2019
4. Dec 01 2019
5. RangeError: Invalid argument.
