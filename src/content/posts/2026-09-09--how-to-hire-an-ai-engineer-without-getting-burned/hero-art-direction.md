# Hero art direction

Thesis: Hire AI engineers by examining evidence of production judgment beyond polished demos.

Mechanisms: interview, trace, scorecard, refund, permission boundary, failed run.

Tensions: presentation versus substance; promises versus observable outcomes; speed versus recovery.

Avoid: robots, brains, neon circuits, corporate laptop stock photography, baked-in titles.

Recommendation: candidate 02, behind-the-facade. Its hidden supports make the central distinction legible without a technical explanation.

## 1. bridge-load-test

File: hero-candidate-01-bridge-load-test.webp

Prompt: Wide 16:9 editorial hero illustration for an executive essay about hiring AI engineers by testing real judgment beyond impressive demos. A small meticulously built steel bridge on an ivory tabletop, supporting three heavy burnt-orange iron blocks at its center. Beneath the bridge the real riveted trusses are clearly visible and structurally plausible, one small brass measurement probe touches its underside. One legible scene about proving the structure can carry the load. Fine art editorial model photography, tactile painted steel, warm limestone background, forest teal bridge, orange weights, directional daylight, restrained sophisticated composition, strong silhouette readable at 200px. Camera low three-quarter view, bridge occupies middle two thirds with generous margins, no people. No text, numbers, logos, watermark, robots, brains, circuitry or futuristic effects.

Square: Recompose as a shorter bridge, keeping weights, trusses, and probe visible.

## 2. behind-the-facade

File: hero-candidate-02-behind-the-facade.webp

Behind the facade are thin wooden braces, rough joinery and one carefully placed teal steel reinforcement. Physical relationship immediately legible, beautiful frontage depends on hidden structure. Cut-paper and balsa wood sculptural editorial photography, warm ivory ground, terracotta side light, dark teal accent, crisp shadows, quietly wry high-end magazine visual, generous negative space. Single structure centered with all critical details in middle two thirds. No words, letters, labels, numbers, logos, watermarks, people, robots, glowing brains or electronics.

Square: Move closer to the corner where ornate facade meets exposed supports; retain both surfaces.
