# Five additional visual directions

These are concepts only; no additional images generated. The selected bridge assets remain attached to the article.

1. **The rehearsal and the storm.** A miniature sailboat moves from still water into a wind-and-wave test tank. A successful demo meets operating conditions. Wide: show the transition across the tank. Square: focus on the bow meeting the first wave.
2. **The underside of the chair.** An elegant chair turned upside down on a cabinetmaker's bench, exposing its joints. Good hiring examines how the work holds together. Wide: chair and a few inspection tools. Square: one clear joint and enough of the chair to identify it. Recommended for its simple silhouette and tactile craft metaphor.
3. **The parachute packing table.** An open parachute, carefully traced suspension lines, and an inspection in progress. Trust comes from preparation you can examine. Wide: canopy, lines and harness arranged across the table. Square: focus on the organized line-to-harness connections.
4. **The dress rehearsal backstage.** A beautiful theater scene viewed from the wings, revealing ropes, counterweights, and emergency controls. The performance depends on unseen engineering. Wide: stage and wings in one cutaway. Square: a curtain edge and a single counterweight mechanism.
5. **The repaired ceramic vessel.** A handsome vessel beside its repair fragments and testing basin. Evidence of failure and recovery can reveal more than a flawless presentation. Wide: vessel and repair evidence. Square: visible repaired seam and water held inside the vessel.

Shared art direction: tactile materials, restrained cream/teal/terracotta palette, clear physical causality, no baked-in words, logos, robots, neon circuitry or stock-photo office scenes.
