## 05-order-effects

Idea number "05". Title "Expose order effects". Main text "Judge the same answers in both orders." Central diagram: top row actual paper cards A then B, labelled "Order A/B", point to result "A wins". Bottom row actual paper cards B then A, labelled "Order B/A", point to result "B wins". Agreement alone does not prove correctness." Make order reversal and its diagnostic purpose unmistakable.
