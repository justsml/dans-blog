## Bottom line

ProductWorld 2027 is the cleanest verified route: it reaches product engineers and engineering leaders, has an open CFP, and does not publish the firsthand-case gate found elsewhere. GIDS 2027 is the strongest thematic match. Its current call explicitly names AI-assisted review, maintainability, developer effectiveness, engineering judgment, system ownership and organization-scale tradeoffs. It also says a talk without real engineering experience is unlikely to fit, so the supplied thought experiments are not enough by themselves.

The talk should be positioned as an engineering operating model, not a forecast of headcount reduction or a generic product-experimentation talk. Its claims are deliberately bounded: easier generation can move constraints toward review, validation and integration, but the degree varies by organization. The 10,000-lines-versus-1,000-lines example is a thought experiment, not measured productivity evidence.

## Fit boundaries and conflicts

- Do not turn the premise into a universal claim that implementation is no longer difficult. Legacy systems, integration and domain constraints still matter.
- Do not claim a measured productivity gain, review ratio, defect reduction or maintenance result unless Dan can supply evidence.
- Do not position the talk as a headcount-reduction forecast. It is about organizational capacity, review, judgment and responsibility.
- Product Engineering is adjacent but distinct: this talk emphasizes organizational constraints, validation and ownership, not customer experimentation.
- GIDS explicitly expects work the speaker directly did or materially contributed to and asks what changed in practice.
- UXDX main stage asks for a real in-house case with decisions, failures and outcomes.
- DX Annual says every speaker led major organizational change and uses real organizational data.
- APQC prefers real organizational problems and meaningful measurable business effects; a teach-a-technique route may be possible but was not confirmed.
- CTO Craft's reachable call page names 2026 while the event shell shows March 2027 dates.
- BACon's 2027 submission page says accepted speakers receive registration to `BACon 2026`; verify the benefit rather than silently correcting it.
- Optimizers Conference is a paid-attendee unconference route, not an open editorial CFP or guaranteed stage.
- CES and POSSIBLE separate editorial applications from commercial or sponsored opportunities. Only editorial routes are in scope.
