## Submission-family guidance

- **CNCF family:** submit one primary to KubeCon Europe, with one materially different backup to Agentics Day or Cloud Native AI + Inference Day. The common rules prohibit a Linux Foundation repeat within the prior year unless materially differentiated and cap main-event speaker listings at three proposals. Do not assume three near-identical submissions are acceptable.
- **ICSA family:** SAIP is the closest talk-shaped route. Working Sessions require a facilitated 90–180 minute collaboration and public deliverable. Tutorials/Briefings require substantial expansion. The SAIP call bars simultaneous review elsewhere and publishes accepted proposals, so check compatibility with other submissions before choosing it.
- **GIDS:** primary angle should be architecture under constraints, not generic agent trends. The official form strongly favors concrete decisions, tradeoffs, and direct experience.
- **Commercial adjacency:** AI Agent Event has an editorial public CFP and explicitly bars commercialism, but it sits inside ITEXPO and markets access to buyers. It remains in the main shortlist, not the paid-placement category, because no pay-to-speak condition was found. Sponsorship is separate.
