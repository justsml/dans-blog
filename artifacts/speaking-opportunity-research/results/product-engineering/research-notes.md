## Constraints and conflicts

- The talk must not claim a demonstrated conversion lift, production deployment, statistically significant result, measured ROI, or customer case. Its demo is synthetic and proves only deterministic policy behavior.
- UXDX main stage asks for a real in-house case with decisions, failures and outcomes. The always-open local stage still asks for a real story, though it does not require prior speaking experience.
- GIDS says a talk deliverable without real engineering experience behind it is unlikely to fit and asks what changed in practice.
- APQC favors real organizational problems and meaningful measurable business effects. A teach-a-technique format may be possible, but this was not confirmed.
- DX Annual says every speaker has led major organizational change and sessions use real organizational data.
- ARC is a new conference and describes its speakers as practitioners running real systems; no deadline or format is public.
- Product-Led Summit's form requires company and business email. Dan's relevant company/affiliation facts are unknown.
- BACon's 2027 submission page says accepted speakers receive complimentary registration to `BACon 2026`. Treat that as a live-source conflict, not a corrected fact.
- KMWorld Europe's page simultaneously says `Opens soon`, supplies an October 9 cutoff, and refers to speaking in 2026 on the 2027 page. It is not actionable until corrected.
- CTO Craft's accessible call page title references 2026 while the event shell shows March 2027 dates.
- Optimizers Conference is not an open keynote CFP. Every paid attendee may lead an unconference discussion. This belongs in a separate paid-attendee category, not the open-CFP queue.
- POSSIBLE routes solution providers to sales. Only its editorial speaker form is in scope; paid placement is excluded.
