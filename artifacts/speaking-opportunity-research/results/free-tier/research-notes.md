## Fit limits and missing information

- Travel origin, budget, preferred regions, compensation threshold, willingness to pay registration, and availability remain unknown.
- Affiliation, company stage, practitioner/end-user status, and access to publishable organizational data remain unknown; these can change FinOps X, SaaStr, and some DevOpsDays eligibility or competitiveness.
- The source deck has an offline synthetic demo, not a proprietary case study. FinOps X and major technical conferences reward measured real-world evidence.
- Avoid named-provider subsidy claims and price predictions. The safer angle is decision mechanics: dependencies, sensitivity ranges, and cost per successful outcome.
- Workshops require new interaction design; the existing 15/30/40/45-minute talk is not automatically a 60–120-minute workshop.
- Speaker compensation, travel support, hotel, registration obligations, recording rights, accessibility, exclusivity, repeat-talk, and simultaneous-submission rules are unknown for most candidates unless captured in `editions.csv`.

Only the FinOps placement is represented as an exclusion row; the other two are recorded as conflict/context evidence rather than qualified editorial editions.
