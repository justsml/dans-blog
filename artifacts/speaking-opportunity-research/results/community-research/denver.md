# Denver and nearby Colorado community speaking routes

Verified: 2026-09-05. This is a local discovery and preparation scan; no organizer was contacted and no event, submission, payment, or reminder was created.

## Best current routes

The strongest low-friction route is the Rocky Mountain AI Interest Group network: it is large, active, explicitly uses community presenters, and has subgroups for Denver, engineering, education, product, ethics, and startups. For a full conference talk, the most actionable current options are ACM Mile-High Video 2027, CITA 2027 for the retrieval/education language angle, and fintech_devcon 2027 if a genuine fintech case can be supplied. Denver Dev Day and DevOpsDays Rockies are strong annual monitoring targets, but their 2026 CFPs are closed.

Topic shorthand used below: **Education** = the two education talks; **Failure** = Automating Improvement From Failure; **Adaptive** = Building Adaptive & Dynamic AI Systems; **Free tier**; **Product** = Future of Product Engineering; **Retrieval**; **Benchmarks**; **Parallelization**; **Judgment**.

Ease is an evidence-based estimate of the route, not acceptance probability: 1 means an organizer message, lightning talk, demo, or discussion is openly invited; 5 means a selective conference or a strong domain/firsthand-evidence requirement.

## Routes to avoid or treat separately

- **TECHSPO Denver 2027:** the public page says only sponsored keynotes remain. That is paid placement, not an editorial CFP, so it should stay outside the submission queue. [Speaker request page](https://techspodenver.com/speaker-request-form/)
- **BLAZE AI Summit 2026:** the September 22 program is already announced and no public CFP was found. It is business-facing and potentially relevant to Free tier, Product, Education, and Judgment, but currently offers only a host-contact route. [Official summit](https://www.blazeaisummit.com/)
- **QCon-style university calendar submissions:** CU Boulder allows public users to submit calendar listings, but the event must be university-affiliated or endorsed and have a department sponsor. Calendar submission is distribution, not an invitation to speak. [CU Boulder calendar rules](https://www.colorado.edu/events/events-calendar-faqs)
- **University public series without an open intake:** CU Boulder’s BioFrontiers “Public Talks for Public Good” has visible recurrence and free public access, but its program is curated and no speaker route is published. [Series page](https://www.colorado.edu/biofrontiers/BioFrontiers-presents)

## Practical ordering

1. **Rehearsal and relationship route:** Denver AI & ML lightning talks, RMAIIG demos, Colorado AI Builders demos, Boulder Ruby, or a Denver DevOps open space.
2. **Full local talk route:** Denver AI Group, AI & Product Colorado, Denver AI Developers, DDSP, or Develop Denver after confirming current programming.
3. **Conference route:** ACM Mile-High Video only with a media-specific case; fintech_devcon only with fintech experience; CITA only with defensible language-access expertise. Monitor Denver Dev Day and DevOpsDays Rockies for their next CFPs.
4. **Public education route:** OLLI at DU is the clearest open proposal system. ATLAS and other CU series are curated relationship routes, not public CFPs.

## Limitations and stopping point

Meetup member totals and displayed attendance are platform counts, not audited unique attendance. Some groups have large memberships but weak recent turnout; both signals are shown rather than collapsed into one popularity claim. Luma’s “going” count is a registration signal, not verified door attendance. University series usually publish schedules and archives but not audience counts or speaker nomination rules.

Searches covered Meetup, Luma, official community sites, local AI/data/dev/product groups, Denver and Colorado Startup Week, annual developer/DevOps conferences, Colorado Springs routes, CU Boulder, CU Denver, University of Denver, and current 2027 regional CFPs. Research stopped when additional results were closed one-off conferences, academic paper-only calls, sponsored speaking, or unrelated professional verticals. The next useful work is choosing two talk/audience pairings and checking the current organizer response path—not adding more weak names.
