# Community speaking routes in preferred US cities

Verified: 2026-09-05. Cities: Boston, New York City, Seattle, San Francisco, San Diego, New Orleans, Las Vegas, Los Angeles, Chicago, Austin, Nashville, Orlando, and Miami. This is discovery and preparation only; no organizer was contacted and no event, proposal, registration, payment, or reminder was created.

## Best bets

For a low-friction first booking, start with **San Diego Python**, **FrontEndParty in New Orleans**, **ACM Austin**, or **Artificial Intelligencers in Nashville**. Each openly invites community speakers and offers a short or flexible format. For a larger product audience, the **ProductTank** chapters in San Francisco, Los Angeles, and Chicago share a public speaker directory. For the deepest AI rooms, use **AI Tinkerers**, but only after converting a talk into a five-minute live demonstration of a system Dan built; these are not slide-talk venues.

Topic shorthand below: **Education** covers *A Skeptic’s Guide to Surviving AI in Education* and *Outsmart Your Lying, Cheating Students*. The remaining labels are **Failure**, **Adaptive**, **Free tier**, **Product**, **Retrieval**, **Benchmarks**, **Parallelization**, and **Judgment**.

Ease estimates the work required to reach the stage, not the chance of acceptance: **1** is an explicit, flexible community invitation; **5** is highly curated, demo-gated, invitation-led, or requires producing the event.

## Multi-city host route

**Tech Week** covers Boston, New York, San Francisco, and Los Angeles. It does not offer a normal speaker CFP; applicants propose and operate an entire event. The public host route accepts formats such as demos, panels, dinners, meetups, and workshops. Its 2026 page says each city contains hundreds of independently hosted events and lists sponsors including Cloudflare, Deel, and Fireworks AI. Boston and New York have passed; San Francisco runs 2026-10-05 through 2026-10-11 and Los Angeles runs 2026-10-12 through 2026-10-18. This is **ease 5** because venue, production, co-hosts, and audience acquisition fall on the applicant. [Official Tech Week page and host route](https://www.tech-week.com/)

## Practical ordering

1. **Fast rehearsal rooms:** San Diego Python, FrontEndParty, Vegas Programmers, ACM Austin, and Artificial Intelligencers.
2. **Product talks with an established audience:** ProductTank Los Angeles, San Francisco, or Chicago. Lead with Product, Judgment, Failure, or Free tier—not a generic AI overview.
3. **Technical credibility rooms:** AI Tinkerers in Boston, NYC, Seattle, San Francisco, San Diego, or Miami. Build a separate five-minute demo; do not compress the existing slide deck and call it a demo.
4. **Large local-tech stages:** New Tech Seattle, Tech Alley Vegas, and Orlando Developers. Their reach is strong, but the speaker path is a conversation with organizers rather than a structured CFP.
5. **Produce the room:** Tech Week or a Refresh Miami calendar event only after deciding to own logistics. These are distribution/hosting routes, not invitations to speak.

## Limitations and stopping point

Meetup member totals and displayed RSVPs are platform counts, not audited attendance. Luma registration numbers are also intent signals, not door counts. Some organizer-managed pages expose a “message” control without publishing email, selection rules, or response expectations. No compensation, travel support, recording license, exclusivity rule, or non-local eligibility should be assumed unless explicitly stated above.

Searches covered first-party Meetup and Luma pages, organizer sites, AI Tinkerers chapters, ProductTank, developer and Python groups, DevOpsDays, Tech Week, and local tech-community hubs in all thirteen cities. Social and Reddit results were used only to discover candidates; none supports a core recommendation. Research stopped at 23 high-signal routes because additional results were inactive groups, one-off vendor events, networking-only gatherings, closed conference calls, paid speaking, or calendars that distribute events without selecting speakers.
