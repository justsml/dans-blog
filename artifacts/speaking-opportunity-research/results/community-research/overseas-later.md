# Overseas community speaking routes for March–June 2027 and later

Research date: 2026-09-05  
Planning window: roughly March–June 2027 and later  
Scope: community, meetup, Luma and community-oriented conference routes outside the United States

No outreach, proposal submission, registration, payment or reminder was performed. Travel origin, budget, preferred regions, compensation threshold, visa eligibility and willingness to self-fund remain unknown.

## How to read this

Most meetups do not publish schedules six to nine months ahead. A route marked `recurring; 2027 date not published` is a real, currently evidenced community with a public speaker or host route, not a confirmed 2027 event. “Ease” measures how direct the speaking route and format match are, not acceptance probability or travel cost:

- 1: standing speaker directory or clear recurring proposal form; little format adaptation
- 2: clear proposal/contact route; modest adaptation or edition timing unknown
- 3: real organizer route but short demo, project requirement or calendar uncertainty
- 4: host/contact or event-submission route without a speaker-specific process
- 5: no current public route; monitoring only

Talk codes: `SE` skeptic/AI education; `EL` evidence/learning; `FI` failure improvement; `AS` adaptive systems; `FT` free tier; `PE` product engineering; `RT` retrieval; `BM` benchmarks; `PA` parallelization; `JU` judgment.

## Practical priority order

1. Use ProductTank digital first for `PE`, `JU` or `FT`; it is the only standing directory designed to let organizers find speakers without committing to travel.
2. For technical talks, build a five-to-seven-minute working demo and target AI Tinkerers London, Paris, Berlin, Amsterdam, Toronto or Singapore. The demo requirement is a real content change, not a shorter slide deck.
3. Use PyData London for a 25-minute `RT`, `BM`, `AS`, `FI` or `PA` variant. Its standing proposal route and monthly cadence make it unusually practical.
4. Treat KubeCon/Agentics in Barcelona and GIDS in Bengaluru as travel anchors. Add meetups only after both the conference and community slot are confirmed.
5. Keep MLOps Community, CNCF chapters, Lorong AI, AI Builders SG and DataTalks.Club as relationship-free monitoring routes until their 2027 calendars or exact speaker process is visible.

## Social and directory signals

No Reddit popularity claim was used to rank a route. Reddit results surfaced community chatter about CNCF and PyData, but first-party member counts, registrations, waitlists, sponsor lists and recurrence were available and used instead. Luma `going`, `went`, `near capacity` and `waitlist` values are platform attendance signals supplied by the event host; they are not audited attendance. Organizer-reported community totals are labeled as such and should not be compared directly with per-event registrations.

## Critical gaps

- Nearly every meetup's March–June 2027 calendar is unpublished.
- Travel origin, budget, visa constraints and willingness to self-fund are unknown, so “reasonable access” refers only to established international hubs, not personal trip feasibility.
- AI Tinkerers requires working technical demos; the ten existing decks are not automatically eligible.
- Several routes use organizer contact rather than a formal CFP. Contacting them would require separate authorization.
- Speaker compensation, travel support, recording rights and exclusivity are generally unstated for community events.
