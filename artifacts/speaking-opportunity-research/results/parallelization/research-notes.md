## Bottom line

KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2027 and Agentics Day are the cleanest topic matches. Their current CFPs explicitly seek multi-agent architecture, AgentOps, production agents, reliability, debugging, and distributed systems. KubeCon also has an exact 30-minute format. Agentics Day needs a 25-minute cut.

GIDS scores highest on the rubric but is conditional: its form requires content grounded in engineering work the speaker directly did or materially contributed to. PlatformCon has a similar, stricter named-organization-and-outcomes requirement. Those are real gates, not proposal-polish issues.

The talk's central distinction must survive every adaptation: independent or competing attempts can gather evidence, but shared models and context create correlated failures. Agreement is not proof. The submission should not imply that redundant agents are independent, that parallel work is automatically faster, or that illustrative budget numbers are measured cost claims.

## Fit boundaries and conflicts

- Keep independent exploration distinct from decomposing separable tasks. The talk should not sell every fan-out topology as the same pattern.
- Never describe same-model workers as statistically independent. Correlated blind spots are part of the thesis.
- The illustrative USD 25, five-minute, 20-attempt controller is an interface example, not a price or performance claim.
- KubeCon permits at most three proposals and generally selects one non-panel session. A prior Linux Foundation version must be materially different, and a prior recording is requested.
- Agentics Day's deadline text pairs `CET` with `UTC+2`; the packet preserves both instead of silently correcting it.
- PlatformCon's Sessionize page and organizer CFP page publish materially different deadline times. Verify before relying on the final day.
- VSLive's official submission page conflicts between PDT metadata and CST body copy. It also normally books speakers for at least two 75-minute sessions.
- SREcon requires the speaker to submit a primarily human-written proposal. Research and clarity assistance do not replace that authorship requirement.
- GIDS requires direct contribution to the engineering work. PlatformCon requires a named implementation and outcomes. Dan's qualifying evidence is unknown.
