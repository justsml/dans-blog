## Claim guardrails

- The transcript's `$5,000–$10,000` and `25–50×` subsidy figures are hypotheses to verify, not stage-ready facts. A safe version compares a measured week of Dan's own subscription usage with contemporaneous public API prices and labels the result **API-rate equivalent**, not provider cost or subsidy.
- Do not infer provider margins from retail prices, quotas, compute commitments, or subscription behavior.
- Treat a tenfold school-budget increase as a stress-test scenario, not a forecast.
- Claims about Chinese, open-weight, local, or specialist models need task-specific evals. Country of origin is not a quality or safety measurement.
- Compare cost per accepted outcome, not token price alone. Include tools, retries, review, recovery, and the human work needed to make an output usable.
