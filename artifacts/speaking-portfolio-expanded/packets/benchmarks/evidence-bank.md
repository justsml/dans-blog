# Evidence: Benchmarks

Sources checked 6 September 2026 are linked beside each slide. This talk owns instrument validity, repeatability, judge agreement, and small-sample inference. Free Tier owns cost per accepted outcome. Retrieval owns the detailed IR history and pooling exercise.

All model scores, costs, and score sequences are teaching fixtures. The published judge article supplies the voice and examples, not a new measured run. Its threshold example is corrected here: fail, fail, pass, pass, pass gives 40% majority disagreement and one adjacent transition.

Twenty independent representative trials with zero failures give an exact one-sided 95% upper bound of 13.91%, approximated by 3/n = 15%. A handpicked corpus does not support that population bound. About one in seven communicates scale, not an estimated failure rate.
