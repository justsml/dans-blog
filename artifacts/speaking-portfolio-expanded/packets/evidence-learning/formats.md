## 5–10 minute lightning talk (slides)

Seven minutes, five slides: 1, 4, 5, 7, 16. This one is a live exercise, not a talk.

## 5–10 minute live demo (AI Tinkerers format)

The qualifying demo is a live tutor session following the hint ladder against a scripted learner, with the observation record written in real time and shown. Show the rubric scoring the transcript and the assistance column filling separately. Requires a tutor you are permitted to show, running against the percentage fixture.

## 60–75 minute workshop

Participants redesign one checkpoint from their own course and leave with a capability statement, a hint ladder, a reconstruction task, a rubric, and an observation record.

**Prerequisites sent in advance:** one checkpoint (a question, problem, or short task) with its current rubric, and the concept it is meant to assess.

| Time | Block | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | Opening | Slides 1–2. Participants write the capability their checkpoint assesses in one sentence, then a second sentence naming what the artifact could hide. |
| 0:10–0:20 | Capability, fixture, smudge | Slides 3–5. Participants write a plausible wrong answer for their concept, one with clean prose and a broken invariant. |
| 0:20–0:35 | Hint ladder | Slide 6. Build five rungs for the wrong answer. Pairs test whether each rung leaves the key move to the learner. |
| 0:35–0:50 | Reconstruction | Slide 7 at full length in the room, then participants write their own transfer item: same invariant, different numbers or context. |
| 0:50–1:05 | Rubric and record | Slide 8. Score the fixture with a three-row rubric. Write the observation record. Facilitator checks for labels creeping in. |
| 1:05–1:15 | AI time, the agent-addressed objective, workload | Slides 9, 12, and 13 compressed. One purposeful follow-up per checkpoint; write the objective as a system prompt; estimate minutes to review a class set. |
| 1:15–1:30 | Pairs worksheet | Slide 15 at full length. |
| 1:30–1:35 | Close | Slide 16. |

**Facilitation notes.** The hint ladder block is where participants discover their hints give away the move. Protect it. If the room skews technical, pair technologists with instructors for the rubric block.

**Artifacts participants leave with:** the capability card, the hint ladder template, the reconstruction and transfer pattern, the rubric, the observation record, the worksheet.
