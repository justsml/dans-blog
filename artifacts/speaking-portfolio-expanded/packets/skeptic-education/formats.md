## 5–10 minute lightning talk (slides)

Seven minutes, four slides: 1, 4, 7, 14.

| Time | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:00 | Two promises, neither useful on Monday. Hold one assignment in mind. |
| 1:00–3:00 | Delegate, Augment, Preserve, with the citation and counter-reading examples. |
| 3:00–5:30 | Gains that vanish and when they don't: Bastani and Kestin in two minutes. |
| 5:30–7:00 | Three sentences to finish. The closing line. |

## 5–10 minute live demo (AI Tinkerers format)

Education talks rarely fit a demo-gated venue. The one qualifying demo is a live run of the four-case local eval set against a real tutor: a confident wrong answer, a right answer with faulty reasoning, a request for the solution, a learner the tutor misunderstands. Show the transcripts and the two-educator scoring sheet. Requires a tutor you are permitted to show and an agreed rubric.

## 60–75 minute workshop

Participants redesign one real assignment and leave with its capability, assistance placement, failure pre-mortem, and pilot plan.

**Prerequisites sent in advance:** one real assignment with its current instructions and rubric, and the learning objective as currently written.

| Time | Block | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | Opening | Slides 1–2. Each participant rewrites their objective as a claim that could fail. |
| 0:10–0:25 | Proxy and capability | Slide 3. Participants name the artifact, the capability, and one piece of evidence for the capability that the artifact does not supply. |
| 0:25–0:40 | Delegate, Augment, Preserve | Slide 4. Participants sort every step of their assignment into a column with a "because students need to learn" sentence. Pairs challenge one placement. |
| 0:40–0:50 | Demo | Slide 5. Run the three switches; participants re-sort one step after watching the verdict change. |
| 0:50–1:05 | Failure modes | Slides 6–9 compressed. Participants mark which of the four their assignment is most exposed to and why. |
| 1:05–1:15 | Pre-mortem | Slide 10 at full length. Pairs. |
| 1:15–1:25 | Pilot plan | Slide 13. Fill the pilot worksheet: baseline sample, rubric, staff time, continuation criterion, two graders. |
| 1:25–1:35 | Share-out and close | Three participants read their three sentences. Slide 14. |

**Facilitation notes.** Mixed rooms of instructors and technologists work well; pair across roles for the challenge step. For rooms over thirty, keep the pre-mortem in pairs and skip the whole-room share.

**Artifacts participants leave with:** the falsifiable-objective card, the DAP worksheet, the four-case eval set template, the pilot worksheet, the vendor questions.
