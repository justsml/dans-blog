## 5–10 minute lightning talk (slides)

Seven minutes using full-deck slides 1, 3, 11, 15.

| Time | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:30 | Conway's law still applies; the organization now includes agents. |
| 1:30–3:00 | Reprice the handoff before copying the org chart. |
| 3:00–5:30 | The table. Activation first, show of hands, then support and urgency. Blocked. |
| 5:30–7:00 | Draw the roster on purpose. Automate the right things, keep the taste. |

## 5–10 minute live demo (AI Tinkerers format)

A qualifying demo runs two agents from the roster live against data you are permitted to show: the research agent produces a competitor or mentions brief with links to sources, the feedback agent clusters a batch of real or realistic tickets and links each cluster to its customers, and the guard script blocks one of two candidate changes with the reason printed. Five minutes. Requires a working crawler, a clustering step, and the gate script.

## 95-minute workshop

Participants redesign one handoff before assigning agents and leave with owners, guards, and a hypothesis-plus-report for one experiment.

**Prerequisites sent in advance:** an org chart or team list for one product group, and one recent product change that was measured, with the metric, the result, and who decided to ship it.

| Time | Block | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | Opening | Full-deck slides 1 and 4. Each participant marks their position on the scale spectrum and names their heaviest process. |
| 0:10–0:25 | Draw the roster | Slide 5. Participants draw artifact contracts between functions before assigning agents. |
| 0:25–0:40 | Name the owners | Slides 5 and 13. For each agent, a person who owns its instructions and mistakes. Pairs check for agents with no owner or owners with too many agents. |
| 0:40–0:50 | Prioritization | Slide 7. Each participant writes what replaced effort in their rubric, or admits nothing has. |
| 0:50–1:00 | Demo | Slide 11 at full length. |
| 1:00–1:10 | Guards | Slide 12. Participants mark rollout, spending, infrastructure, customer messaging and deletion boundaries on their roster and name who holds each guard. |
| 1:10–1:20 | Beta enrollment | Slide 9. Rewrite the pre-work change as a targeted opt-in beta: which cluster asked, who is similar, what the invitation says. |
| 1:20–1:30 | Hypothesis and report | Slide 14. Write the hypothesis and the automatic report for that change. |
| 1:30–1:35 | Close | Slide 15. |

**Facilitation notes.** The owner step is where most rooms discover that several functions have no owner today, agent or not. For engineering-heavy rooms, extend the guards block and shorten prioritization.

**Artifacts participants leave with:** the agent roster with owners, the guard list, the beta invitation, the hypothesis and report.
