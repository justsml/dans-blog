## Corrections to the review proposal

Unjudged documents are not irrelevant forever. Some scorers treat them as nonrelevant; new judgments and evaluation methods change the result. Deep, diverse TREC pools can remain reusable, supported by the 2022 TREC-8 recheck. The synthetic pool is intentionally shallow and is not evidence against all shared collections.

A generator can be designed to abstain. Historical IR also had automated downstream consumers. The talk concerns the retrieval-to-generation handoff, not a claim that every prior system required a person reading a list.

McDermott’s Artificial Intelligence 19(1), 39–88 paper is from 1982, not 1980. The talk does not claim XCON failed because nobody could tell when it was wrong, or reduce the expert-system industry’s decline to one cause.

Story: supply a query whose new relevant result was absent from your expected set. Retain the original labels and the rejudgment. No personal incident was invented.
