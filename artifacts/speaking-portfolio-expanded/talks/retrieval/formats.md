# Retrieval formats

The [hand-authored browser deck](../../../../public/decks/retrieval/index.html) uses `?route=40` (default), `?route=30`, or `?route=15`. Route selection changes slide order membership, timing and speaker notes before Reveal starts. Add `&all` only for review/export: it reveals all answers. No runtime model or stage script is required.

| Slot | Route | Included interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 minutes | [Script](script-15min.md), [adaptation](adaptation-15min.md), `?route=15` | Eight slides; 60-second pair judgment, prediction and P@2 reveal, one next-check reflection. |
| 30 minutes | [Script](script-30min.md), [adaptation](adaptation-30min.md), `?route=30` | Fourteen slides; two-minute passage slide; judgment and pooling exercise protected. |
| 40 minutes | [Outline](index.md), [script](script-40min.md), `?route=40` | Fifteen slides; early exercise before historical diagnosis; one-minute corpus ownership beat. |
| 45 minutes | 40-minute route plus five Q&A minutes | No extra slides. |

Timings include spoken delivery, reading, responses and transitions. [Per-slide budgets](timing.md) are desk estimates, not rehearsal measurements. The only Story slot is an optional replacement, never extra time. Use [contracts.md](contracts.md) as the presenter’s fixture card. Keep F hidden until slide 10 and do not use `?all` for a live exercise.
