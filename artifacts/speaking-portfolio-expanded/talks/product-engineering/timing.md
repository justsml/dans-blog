# Product Engineering timing budgets

Rehearsal budgets, not measured delivery. Spoken words include bridges; exclude headings, screens, sources and directions. Subtract explicit vote, reading, silence, writing, arithmetic and response budgets before calculating speech density. Optional Story material replaces existing prose after a rehearsal; it does not extend the route. The authored walkthrough is counted, not excluded as a zero-word demo.
