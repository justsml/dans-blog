# Agent interfaces and approval boundaries

Research emits dated sources and a brief. Feedback emits clusters linked to original complaints and customer context. Product review reconciles disagreement and emits an accepted hypothesis. Build consumes that hypothesis and returns a candidate plus evidence. The experiment owner chooses a bounded rollout and receives its report.

A person owns each interface, including malformed or misleading inputs and recovery after failed delivery. Budget review capacity. Collaboration while discovering a contract should not become an endless all-agent conversation.

Approval boundaries include expanding a cohort, spending beyond the run budget, creating infrastructure, removing infrastructure, sending customer messages, and deleting customer records. The tool layer enforces those boundaries before execution.

Support <=5% and no fabricated urgency are the fixed synthetic policy. Passing means eligible for human review. The stage sequence has one duration per route: 5:00 for 40/30 and 3:30 for 15.
