# Bullet outlines

One compressed sheet per talk: the spine in a screen, then each slide's on-screen lines, the topic sentence of every spoken paragraph, and its Story, delivery and source notes. Use them to rehearse the arc, to fill a CFP form's outline field, and to find the slide that owns an idea.

Each talk's `index.md` is the canonical complete talk; the sheets are hand-written and follow it.
