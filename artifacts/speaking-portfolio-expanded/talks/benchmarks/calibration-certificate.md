# Calibration Certificate

Suite: ______ · candidate: ______ · baseline: ______ · scorer/rubric: ______ · corpus: ______ · owner/date: ______

This is a worksheet for testing an eval suite, not an accreditation or a claim of general reliability. Attach the evidence; an empty row remains an open question.

| Question | Evidence to attach | Claim or limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Validity: what decision does this score support? | Acceptance criteria and counterexamples; an independent state read for cancellation | An authorized state change is observable. A claim about broad capability needs a theory and evidence connecting tasks to the construct. |
| Reliability: does repeating the measurement change the decision? | Repeated verdicts, threshold, model/prompt/scorer versions | Define majority disagreement separately from adjacent transitions. |
| Agreement: which errors does the grader miss? | Independent labels, confusion matrix, disagreement reasons, order/identity checks | A high agreement rate can conceal all critical failures. |
| Power: what difference could this experiment detect? | Sampling plan, counts, uncertainty and power analysis for the intended comparison | Twenty selected regressions specify behavior; they do not establish a population rate. |
| Contamination: what evidence influenced development? | Provenance, exposure and tuning log; separately collected release evidence | A filename cannot make reused development data a holdout. |
| Goodhart: what exploit improves the score? | A deliberately persuasive wrong answer and the instrument's verdict | A nice paragraph cannot compensate for an unauthorized action. |
