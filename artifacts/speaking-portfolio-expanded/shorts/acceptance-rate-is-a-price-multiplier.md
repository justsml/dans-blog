# Doubling on a Tuesday

3 min · video · parent: [Buy Me a Free Tier](../outlines/free-tier-40min.md), slide 10

Everyone watches the token price. The number that actually moves your unit cost is on no dashboard.

## Hook

Define the denominator before any dollar figure. An accepted outcome meets a rule you wrote down. An impressive answer a human quietly fixed afterward is not a success; it's a success plus a hidden salary.

## Beat: the division

Fixture, deliberately small: 1,000 attempts a month, three cents each all-in, 75% accepted. $30 for 750 outcomes, four cents each. Now divide by the acceptance rate and read it as a multiplier on sticker price. 75% → you pay 1.33× list per result you keep. 45% → 2.22×.

## Beat: the comparison

Dropping from 75% to 45% acceptance costs exactly what every token price in your stack doubling overnight would cost. One of those is front-page news and a vendor apology. The other is a Tuesday, a prompt change, a model upgrade nobody evaluated, and it never shows up as a line item.

## Landing

Put cost per accepted outcome in the design doc next to the latency budget. Then multiply by your volume before you feel anything; at a thousand attempts a month this is a curiosity, at a million it's a hiring plan.

## On screen

`cost per accepted = spend ÷ accepted`. Then: `1 ÷ 0.75 = 1.33×` · `1 ÷ 0.45 = 2.22×`. Big: **same as doubling every price**.

## Scope

Synthetic fixture. Nobody outside a provider knows a margin; this short never estimates one.
