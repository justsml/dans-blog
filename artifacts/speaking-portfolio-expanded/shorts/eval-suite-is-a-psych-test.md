# Your Eval Suite Needs Therapy

3 min · video · parent: [Stop Looking at My Benchmarks](../outlines/benchmarks-40min.md), slides 4 and 5

It produces scores. You make decisions from the scores. Nobody has ever checked whether it measures anything.

## Hook

Your eval suite is a psychometric instrument. Tasks in, judgments out, a number you use to ship or not ship. Psychometrics has a word for checking whether the instrument measures what you think: validity. Where's yours?

## Beat: three cases it should split

Cancellation workflow. Case one: a warm, articulate reply, and the account is still active. Case two: correct cancellation, awkward wording. Case three: an unauthorized cancellation with a perfect explanation. If your grader only reads the chat, case one beats case two and case three passes. You do not have an eval. You have a tone detector with a pass rate.

## Beat: constraints versus preferences

Cronbach and Meehl called it construct validity: what property does the score actually track, and what evidence says so? Separate constraints (right account, authorized state change) from preferences (tone). Average them into one number and an unauthorized action buys its way out with a nice paragraph.

## Landing

Write the counterexample that would embarrass the grader. Keep it after it ruins the chart. The suite is an instrument; test the instrument before you trust the reading.

## On screen

Three rows: *sounds great / account active* → PASS?, *correct / awkward* → FAIL?, *unauthorized / eloquent* → PASS? Big red question mark on the grader.

## Source

Cronbach and Meehl (1955), Construct validity in psychological tests. Messick (1990), Validity of Test Interpretation and Use.

## Story slot

The green eval run followed by the production failure. The score, the observed state, the assumption the scorer missed.
