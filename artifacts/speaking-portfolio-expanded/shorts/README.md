# Shorts

One idea, one number or one picture, one landing line. These are the cuts of the long talks that stand on their own: 1 to 5 minute videos, and 5 to 10 minute lightning slots. Every file names its parent talk and slide so the evidence bank, the fixtures and the disclaimers travel with it.

Rules that carry over from the long talks: keep the joke, one scope line if the numbers are fixtures, first-hand story slot named explicitly, sources at the bottom and not on the screen.
