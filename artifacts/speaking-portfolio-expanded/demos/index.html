The objective changes the boundary

A fix needs a promotion gate

A recurring local database error can become a readiness check. Similarity finds a prior case. It does not establish the cause of the current failure.

Architecture as a bounded decision

Change the task, then tighten the budget or deadline. The policy either finds an allowed strategy or stops. It never silently spends past the cap.
