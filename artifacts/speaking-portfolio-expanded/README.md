## What makes the talks distinct

| Talk | Central audience decision | Worked example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skeptic's Guide | Which human capability should this assignment develop? | The objective changes whether assistance belongs in Delegate, Augment or Preserve. |
| Improvement From Failure | Where should a recurring lesson become durable behavior? | A local connection failure becomes a proposed readiness check, with three promotion gates. |
| Adaptive Systems | Which strategy deserves the available budget and authority? | A known lookup, a routine task and a novel failure receive different bounded strategies. |
| Free Tier | Which assumptions depend on today's price? | A fixed workload under 1×, 2×, 5× and 10× inference prices. |
| Evidence of Learning | What follow-up would distinguish understanding from a polished artifact? | Reconstruction and transfer, plus a scripted learner replay. |
| Product Engineering | Which faster learning loops still honor the product promise? | An activation winner violates support and false-urgency constraints. |

Sources appear in the relevant slide notes and outlines. The evidence notes distinguish empirical findings, guidance, proposed practices and illustrations. One education paper has correction metadata whose full correction text was inaccessible during research; its evidence entry records that limitation and the talk avoids numeric effect-size claims.

The offline kit uses scripted or deterministic fixtures. It makes no model calls, estimates no provider subsidy, grades no student, and deploys no experiment. The interactive checks demonstrate the stated policies. They do not establish educational benefit or production performance. The kit includes tests and a browser-ready entry point, with no setup or credentials needed.

## Reuse across the portfolio

Pair the strategic education talk with the assessment-design talk when a program wants both framing and implementation. Pair failure improvement with adaptive systems when the audience wants to retain lessons and change execution policy. Pair the economics talk with Product Engineering when the discussion spans market incentives and organizational decisions.

The original eight decks remain in the sibling `flagship-talks` directory. The new talks share those engineering principles while serving different audiences and decisions.
