## Additional verification exposed by the audit

The sandbox paragraph's absolute “cannot leak in bulk / one tenant” was false as an architectural guarantee. It now says scoped workers can limit exposure while shared credentials, control planes, backups and exports can still expose many tenants. Mandatory patch policy and support windows remain with the owner. A sandbox per user is one option, not a universal prerequisite.

The plan-space arithmetic is exact under stated assumptions: 6!×3⁶=524,880; 16×0.08×0.92/0.02²=2,944; floor(48,000/2,944)=16; 524,880/16=32,805. But the audit overaccepted the statistical interpretation: ordinary WAU is not independent sample size, a baseline-variance heuristic is not an exact 8%-versus-10% power computation, and sixteen naive arm slots are not a multiplicity-corrected design. contracts.md now spells out eligible unique users, assignment unit, independent binary outcomes, variance, control and stopping-rule assumptions. The ratio illustrates one allocation; it cannot establish that all alternatives need independent tests or that reasoning models cannot generalize.
