## Evidence and disputed audit claims

- [Voorhees, Soboroff and Lin 2022, primary abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11086): new pools include three transformer models, two BM25 baselines, and previously noncontributing TREC-8 submissions. All-run rankings by mean score have tau correlations above 0.99. Does not generalize to every shallow private pool.
- [BEIR 2021 v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08663): 18 datasets; robust BM25 baseline and higher-cost reranking/late-interaction results in that experiment. No current leaderboard inference.
- [Spärck Jones 1972 paper record](https://doi.org/10.1108/eb026526): term specificity, Journal of Documentation 28(1), 11–21. Not a claim that modern hybrid search was invented in 1972.
- Crossref publisher metadata rechecked Spärck Jones; Salton/Wong/Yang 1975, CACM 18(11), 613–620; Salton/Allan/Buckley 1993, 49–58; Voorhees 2000, IP&M 36(5), 697–716; McDermott 1982, AI 19(1), 39–88. Crossref returned HTTP 429 on Furnas and Zobel; primary paper/repository records were used rather than claiming every API request succeeded.
- [Zobel author-hosted 1998 paper](https://people.eng.unimelb.edu.au/jzobel/fulltext/sigir98.pdf), [LSA paper](https://www.cs.csustan.edu/~mmartin/LDS/Deerwester-et-al.pdf), and [SIGIR museum](https://sigir.org/resources/museum/) support the preserved historical references. The Cranfield question-count disagreement was removed from the teaching artifact, not “settled” by selecting a preferred secondary count.
- Small fixture counts are arithmetic over authored examples. Four of twelve differences is descriptive of those twelve marks; no sampling/population statement follows.
